## Scope and mode

- Target: `app/page.tsx`, the public Bruno landing page.
- Mode: Persuade.

## Visitor and job

- Solo SaaS founders who are already juggling revenue, customers, product, support, and shipping across a predictable stack.
- Help them understand in one viewport that Bruno is a business operator with persistent company context, not another chatbot or hosted agent.
- Invite them into the shipped dashboard and direct agent-creation flow; keep sign-in and the public build available without presenting the pivot capabilities as shipped.

## Proof and constraints

- Demonstrate the Business Graph, Action Inbox, explicit policies, six operating loops, and verified outcome cycle with clearly labeled illustrative data.
- Do not fabricate customers, testimonials, pricing, measured results, or product availability.
- Make `/dashboard` and `/agents#create-agent-title` explicit landing-page destinations while preserving `/sign-in` and the existing authenticated control plane.
- Keep the public page useful and legible on mobile, with keyboard-visible interactions and non-color status labels.

## Direction and memorable moment

- Chosen direction: The Company Daybook.
- World: a founder's annotated operating notebook crossed with a management control ledger; grid-ruled stock, adhesive index tabs, blue editorial pencil, citron status highlights, and a dark ink spine.
- First viewport: an oversized dated spread with Bruno's promise, a primary dashboard action, and direct agent creation on the left; three founder decisions sit on the right and a Business Graph line crosses the fold.
- Signature interaction: as the visitor follows the operating loop, the ledger's blue route advances from business change through verification while each stage remains visible without animation.

- Replace all illustrative company records with validated product captures when the new command center ships.
