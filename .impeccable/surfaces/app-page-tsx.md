## Scope and mode

- Target: `app/page.tsx`, the public Bruno.Ai landing page.
- Mode: Persuade.

## Visitor and job

- Solo SaaS founders juggling revenue, customers, product, support, and shipping across a predictable stack.
- Make Bruno.Ai legible in one viewport as a 24/7, always-running AI agent that learns from the founder and improves through interaction, not another chatbot or hosted-agent console.
- Invite the visitor into the shipped dashboard and direct agent-creation flow while keeping sign-in and the public build available.

## Proof and constraints

- `design/a.png` is the binding visual reference for the Bruno.Ai mark, Satoshi/Inter pairing, ivory/stone base, charcoal/espresso structure, mint/lime signals, orbital pattern, and Action Inbox grammar.
- Demonstrate the Business Graph, Action Inbox, explicit policies, six operating loops, founder-feedback learning, and verified outcome cycle with clearly labeled illustrative data.
- Do not fabricate customers, testimonials, pricing, measured results, or product availability.
- Make `/dashboard` and `/agents#create-agent-title` explicit destinations while preserving `/sign-in` and the existing authenticated control plane.
- Preserve keyboard-visible interaction, non-color status labels, and a complete mobile reading order without horizontal overflow.

## Direction and memorable moment

- Chosen direction: Calm Operations Brandboard, user-pinned hard reference; seed `b32744ed`.
- World: high-air ivory fields, precise charcoal typography, warm structural rules, mint/lime state signals, circular company-system geometry, softly rounded product surfaces, and sparse ambient depth.
- First viewport: an always-on 24/7 promise and clear dashboard/agent actions sit beside a faithful illustrative Action Inbox; the orbital signal pattern binds brand, learning, and product proof.
- Signature interaction: one mint signal travels around the Business Graph orbit while every state remains visible; reduced-motion clients keep the complete static model.
- Rollout boundary: this landing page is the shipped visual ground truth; authenticated surfaces migrate deliberately in later phases and must not be described as already converted.
